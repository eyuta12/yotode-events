Ethiopian Orthodox Wedding Ceremonies

Betrothal (Emanget)

The formal agreement between families where rings are exchanged. We ensure this sacred moment is celebrated with traditional customs and modern elegance.

  • ✓ Family coordination
  • ✓ Traditional refreshments
  • ✓ Venue decoration
  • ✓ Photography & videography

Church Wedding

The sacred union blessed by the Ethiopian Orthodox Church. We coordinate with your priest, arrange ceremonial details, and ensure a spiritually meaningful celebration.

  • ✓ Church coordination
  • ✓ Ceremonial clothing guidance
  • ✓ Wedding procession planning
  • ✓ Professional documentation

Wedding Reception & Feast

A grand celebration with traditional Ethiopian cuisine, music, and dancing. Our team coordinates all catering, entertainment, and ambiance for a memorable feast.
